About

I'm a First-Class Geography graduate from the University of Bristol, looking for a graduate role in GIS, land surveying or construction. What ties these together for me is spatial data: collecting it accurately in the field, analysing it, and seeing it put to use on the ground.

I have practical experience processing and interpreting satellite imagery, aerial photography, coastal survey data and environmental datasets in QGIS, ArcGIS and R. My final-year dissertation compared multiple shoreline datasets at Seaton, evaluated their reliability and combined them to map coastal change and risk.

My wider project work covers species distribution modelling and atmospheric modelling. I also have hands-on land surveying experience, from topographic surveys and setting out to processing field data into usable site surveys.
